India is a beautiful country located in South Asia.
It is known for its rich culture, history, traditions, and diversity.
New Delhi is the capital of India.
The country has 28 states and 8 Union Territories.
India is famous for its historical monuments, such as the Taj Mahal, Red Fort, and India Gate.
Hindi and English are widely used, while many regional languages are spoken across the country.
India celebrates festivals like Diwali, Holi, Eid, and Durga Puja with great joy. It is also known for its delicious food, beautiful landscapes, and unity in diversity.
